# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct. We pledge to make participation in our
community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

The full text is available at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Standards

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on what is
best for the community. Behaviour that the Contributor Covenant identifies as
unacceptable — including harassment, personal attacks, and other disruptive
conduct — will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to the project
maintainers at **kontakt@allegroit.dk**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy and security will be
respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a temporary or
permanent ban from the project's spaces.
